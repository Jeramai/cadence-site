Privacy policy

Draft of 14 August 2026. This document is a plain statement of how the product is built. It has not yet been reviewed by counsel and is not a contract.

The short version

Cadence processes camera images on your phone and nowhere else. No frame is uploaded, written to storage, or made visible to your employer or to us. What leaves the device is the outcome, meaning a count of repetitions, seconds held, and the points those earned.

Camera data

When you start a camera session, the app runs a pose model locally. The model turns each frame into a set of body landmark coordinates, uses them to decide whether a repetition or a hold occurred, and then discards the frame. The app requests camera permission for this and for nothing else.

  • No video or still image is recorded.
  • No frame or landmark set is transmitted off the device.
  • No frame is retained after it has been processed.
  • There is no remote server involved in counting.

Health data

If you allow it, Cadence reads steps, stand hours, mindful minutes, and workouts from Apple Health or Android Health Connect, so that activity you did outside the app counts toward your ring. Cadence reads these values, converts them into points, and does not share the underlying health records with your employer. You can withdraw this permission at any time in your device settings, and the ring continues to work without it.

What your employer can see

Your employer receives aggregate and anonymised figures only. Typically this means how many people closed a ring, participation rates over time, and team challenge totals.

  • Your employer cannot see your individual health data.
  • Your employer cannot see which exercises you did, or how well you did them.
  • Your employer cannot see camera derived form or posture information.
  • Your employer cannot see your weight, your goals, or your reminders.

Where data is stored

Cadence runs one database project per customer company, in a region that company chooses. Your records are not stored in a shared table alongside another employer's records. On the device, your ring state and settings are held in the operating system secure store.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, correction of it, export of it, or erasure of it. Because your employer is the data controller for the deployment you use, the fastest route is normally through them, and we support them in answering. You may also contact us directly using the address below.

Retention

Ring history is kept for the life of the customer contract. When a contract ends we provide an export and then delete the entire database project. Camera frames are never retained at any point, so there is nothing to delete.

Contact

Write to privacy@cadence.example with any question about this policy or about a specific deployment.
